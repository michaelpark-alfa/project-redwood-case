NOTES
1. NRR (mgmt) = trailing-twelve-month ARR of the customer cohort active twelve months earlier, divided by that cohort's opening ARR. Per the management workbook, the calculation includes contracted price uplifts effective the following month and excludes churn from customers lost through M&A or portfolio sale ("non-controllable churn"). December reflects the Meridian Property Group and Harborview Commercial Partners Multi-Site Addenda (+$2.0M combined) signed 19 December 2025.
2. Churn is recorded in the month the contract ends, not the month notice is given. Notice was received for the September churn (Ashgrove Realty) in June 2025.
3. New-logo ARR in Q4 includes year-end promotional pricing (see Board minutes, 11 December 2025).
4. Expansion outside the December addenda is recorded at a straight-line $0.05M per month. Revenue Operations allocates annual price uplifts evenly; actual uplift timing follows renewal dates.
