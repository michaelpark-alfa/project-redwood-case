3. FACILITIES
Term Loan:          $52.0M, fully drawn at close.
Revolving Facility: $5.0M, undrawn at close. Available for working capital and general corporate purposes.
Maturity:           Term Loan 6 years from close; Revolver 5 years.
Amortisation:       1.0% per annum, paid quarterly; balance at maturity.

4. PRICING
Interest:           Term SOFR + 5.50% per annum, with a 1.00% SOFR floor.
PIK toggle:         For the first 8 full fiscal quarters after close, the Borrower may elect to pay up to 2.50% of the margin in kind (capitalised to principal).
OID:                2.0%.
Undrawn fee:        0.50% on the Revolver.
Call protection:    102 in year 1, 101 in year 2, par thereafter.
Indicative 3-month Term SOFR as of 20 May 2026: 4.30%. Illustrative all-in cash coupon at close: 9.80% (7.30% if the full PIK toggle is elected).

5. FINANCIAL COVENANTS
(a) Recurring Revenue Leverage (tested quarterly until conversion):
    Total Debt / Recurring Revenue not to exceed:
      Close to Q4 2026        3.25x
      Q1 2027 – Q2 2027       3.00x
      Q3 2027 – Q4 2027       2.75x
    "Recurring Revenue" means the annualised value of contracted subscription fees from customers that are live or scheduled to go live within 90 days, EXCLUDING implementation, onboarding, data-migration and other one-time fees, and EXCLUDING any contract (or addendum) under which the customer has delivered notice of termination.

(b) Conversion: from the first test date after 31 December 2027 (or earlier at the Borrower's election once LTM EBITDA exceeds $6.0M), the Recurring Revenue Leverage covenant is replaced by Total Net Leverage (Total Debt less unrestricted cash / LTM Consolidated EBITDA) not to exceed 7.00x, stepping down by 0.50x every two quarters to 5.50x.

(c) Minimum Liquidity: unrestricted cash plus undrawn Revolver commitments of not less than $3.0M at all times, tested monthly.

(d) Equity cure: up to 4 cures over the life of the facility, not more than 2 in any 4 consecutive quarters.

6. CONSOLIDATED EBITDA DEFINITION (KEY POINTS)
Add-backs for stock-based compensation, transaction costs and restructuring costs are permitted. Run-rate cost savings are permitted up to 15% of EBITDA (before such add-back), realisable within 12 months. Capitalised software development costs are NOT deducted.

7. OTHER KEY TERMS
- Security: first-priority lien on substantially all assets, including IP and pledged equity.
- Reporting: monthly ARR schedule and KPI pack within 30 days of month-end; quarterly financials within 45 days; annual audited financials within 120 days.
- Change of control: an Event of Default.
- Conditions precedent include: receipt of all material third-party consents (including customer change-of-control consents identified in legal diligence), final quality of earnings report, and no Material Adverse Change.
