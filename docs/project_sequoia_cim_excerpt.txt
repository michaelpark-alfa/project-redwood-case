COMPANY OVERVIEW
Sequoia Health Systems Inc. ("Sequoia" or the "Company") is a vertical SaaS platform for revenue-cycle management at independent physician groups and ambulatory surgery centres. Founded in 2016, the Company provides AI-assisted medical coding, claims scrubbing, denial management and patient billing tools. Sequoia operates on a subscription model with multi-year contracts and a 120-day implementation cycle, plus a usage-based fee per claim processed.

CAPITALIZATION (PRE-TRANSACTION)
The Company has a $6.0M term loan from a regional bank, repaid at close. Existing equity is held by the founders (48%) and a growth equity investor (52%). The proposed transaction is a sponsor-led buyout with unitranche financing of approximately $17M. Pro-forma net leverage is expected at approximately 5.5x Adjusted EBITDA.

AI PRODUCT NOTE
Management describes Sequoia as "AI-native". The coding assistant uses a large language model to suggest CPT and ICD-10 codes from clinical notes; coders accept or edit suggestions. Per the CIM, AI-assisted coding is used on approximately 70% of claims processed and has reduced first-pass denial rates from 11% to 7%. Management notes that the coding model uses a third-party LLM API with a retrieval layer over payer rules; it is not fine-tuned.

KEY RISKS IDENTIFIED BY THE COMPANY
- Reimbursement policy changes (CMS physician fee schedule).
- Concentration: largest customer represents 9% of ARR; top 10 represent 38%.
- Dependency on a single clearinghouse partner for claims submission.
- Regulatory: HIPAA compliance; the Company holds HITRUST certification.
