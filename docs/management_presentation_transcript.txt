PART 1 — COMPANY OVERVIEW (Dana Whitlock)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
WHITLOCK: Thanks, everyone, for coming. I'll start with why Redwood exists. Commercial real estate runs on leases, and lease data is a mess. A typical mid-market operator has a few thousand leases and amendments, spread across PDFs, scanned documents and spreadsheets. When that data is wrong, people miss rent escalations, miss option dates, and report the wrong numbers to their lenders. We built Redwood to fix that.

We have three products. Lease Abstraction, which is our AI engine — it reads every lease and turns it into structured data. Portfolio Analytics, which sits on top of that data. And Tenant Communications, which automates notices and reconciliation letters to tenants. Customers almost always start with Abstraction and expand into the other two.

We sell annual subscriptions, priced per property per month, with a 90-day implementation. We ended 2025 with $18.2 million of ARR, 142 customers, 34% growth, and net revenue retention of 118%. Gross margin is 71%.

I want to be clear about one thing: we are an AI-native company. AI is not a feature we bolted on. Our model is fully proprietary — we own every weight — and it's trained on the largest corpus of commercial leases in the industry.
